# M25 — Custom assets (outline, 2026-10-05)

*Outline only: expanded into a full plan (goal, done-when, design decisions, lettered steps, risks) when the
milestone starts (`milestone-workflow`). Part of the 1.1 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md) (order and shared
decisions). Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13.*

- **Guidance:** `Docs/CustomAssets.md`: the art contract (`ArtContract.cs`: 1×1 pivot, facing +Z to the street,
  height, `Kit.mat` or own URP Simple Lit material), how to add a growable variant (an `AgeVisualSet` slot) vs a
  placeable building (`BuildingDefinition` + prefab + `RequiredTech`), import settings, testing in Play mode.
- **Editor wizard:** *CityBuilder › Add Custom Building…*: pick a model / prefab, choose growable (age × zone ×
  level × density) or placeable (cost, upkeep, effects), it writes the assets, validates against the art contract
  and adds it to the database.
- **Searchable placement menu:** a panel (hotkey + toolbar button) listing every unlocked placeable and decoration
  with a search box, filters by group / age, and custom items tagged "Custom". Custom growables can optionally be
  *placed* as a ploppable at a chosen level (a "landmark" that counts as that zone's capacity). Builds on M20's
  groups.
