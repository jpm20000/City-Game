# M22 — Road layout (plan, 2026-10-05)

*Part of the 1.1 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md) (order and shared decisions). Where it fits:
`../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13 (Grid, Roads, Traffic, Save / load, UI).*

**Save:** v8.

## Goal
Two feedback items about roads: an **Avenue is two tiles wide** (a carriageway with a median, not a thicker street)
and **highways are one-way** (a direction per cell; traffic and vehicles follow it). Both stay opt-in, so a city
that never lays a paired avenue or a one-way highway plays exactly as in 1.0.

## Done when
- The Avenue tool lays a 2-cell strip along the drag (second lane on the drag's right, flipped with R), refuses
  where the pair does not fit, upgrades an existing street only together with its neighbour lane, and demolishing
  one lane demolishes the pair. The median shows between the lanes.
- The Highway tool lays one-way cells in the direction of the drag (R = two-way); arrows show the direction;
  SelectionPanel can reverse a stretch or make it two-way; commute traffic and the cosmetic vehicles obey it.
- Traffic is a directed graph for one-way cells only: an all-two-way city gives bit-identical flow (the age-less
  sim and every recorded baseline hold); one-way flow, ramps and unreachable sinks are tested.
- Save v8 (`RoadDirections`, `RoadPairs`) with a migration step and test; the v1 fixture still migrates;
  save → load → continue = uninterrupted run with one-way highways and paired avenues in it.
- `PerfBenchmark` re-measured (directed Dial's on 96²) or the gap stated; UI-only play-through passes; docs updated.

## Design decisions (defaults the user can change)
| Topic | Default | Why / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Avenue model | Still **one road cell per tile** (tier Avenue); a *pair* is two Avenue cells that point at each other through a per-cell `RoadPairs` byte (0 none, else the side of the partner: N / E / S / W) | A real 2-cell road object would change `GridData`'s one-road-per-cell model everywhere (Roadmap decision). The pair byte is what lets "demolish one lane = both" and the median art work without guessing which neighbours belong together (two avenues side by side stay two pairs). |
| Existing avenues | Unpaired avenue cells stay valid (single lane, today's look and numbers): old saves, the engaged player's upgrade-in-place and the `SetRoadTier` API are untouched | Keeps the baselines and the migration trivial (v7 → v8 = empty layers). |
| Laying a pair | Paint-while-held as now; the drag axis comes from the step between two painted cells, the partner is the cell to the right of the direction of travel (R flips the side). The first cell is placed once the second shows the axis, or on release along the last axis (default +x). The partner must be free or a lower-tier road; otherwise the cell is refused with the reason in the hint ("Needs the lane beside it: blocked by …") | Per-cell painting keeps the existing drag code, one insufficient-funds toast per drag, and the audio. A line-preview tool would be a second interaction model. |
| Avenue price / capacity | **Unchanged per lane** ($150, capacity 400, travel 3, upkeep 3): a pair costs and carries twice one cell. (Decided in 22b instead of the draft re-tune: each lane is a real tile of land, the flow already spreads over parallel cells, and leaving the numbers alone keeps the engaged player and every balance baseline as measured in M21.) | A re-tune stays a one-line change in `gen_age_content.py` `ROAD_TIERS` if play-testing wants a cheaper pair. |
| Frontage | Unchanged: any road tier with frontage gives access; the inner lane of a pair simply has the other lane on its far side | No new rule. |
| One-way | **Highway cells only** carry a direction byte `RoadDirections` (0 two-way, 1..4 = N / E / S / W = the way you may leave the cell); drag direction sets it, **R** on the Highway tool toggles one-way / two-way (default one-way), SelectionPanel has **Reverse** (the connected stretch of one-way cells on the same axis) and **Make two-way** | The point of the feature is one-way highways, so the drag default is one-way; two-way stays one key away. Other tiers cannot be directed (Roadmap decision). |
| Movement rule | A step from cell A to adjacent B is legal if A is two-way or points at B, **and** B is two-way or does not point at A (no head-on entry; entering from the back or the side is a ramp). Ramps = any one-way cell touching another road; no interchanges in 1.1 | Same rule in traffic and vehicles, one pure helper (`RoadDirections.CanStep`). |
| Connectivity | `RoadNetwork` (map-edge entry, road access, power / water along roads) stays **undirected** | Only travel is directed; a one-way highway still connects the network, powers its neighbours' roads and counts for access like any road. |
| Map-edge exits | A map-edge road seeds the outside sink only if it is two-way or points off the map; a one-way cell pointing inward is not an exit | Otherwise cars could "leave" the wrong way down a one-way highway. |
| Unreachable | A home whose frontage road cannot reach any sink (all routes blocked by direction) puts no trips on the roads, as with an unreachable road today; the SelectionPanel commute line says "no route" | The statistical model has no teleport; the player sees it and fixes the direction. |
| Highway upgrade | Dragging a highway over a road upgrades it as now; the new highway cell takes the drag direction (or two-way with R); a highway cell already there keeps its direction (use Reverse / two-way) | Re-dragging must not silently flip someone's carefully laid direction. |

## Architecture
- **Pure (Grid asmdef):** `GridData` gains `byte[] m_RoadDirections` and `byte[] m_RoadPairs` with
  `GetRoadDirection / SetRoadDirection` (only on tier 5; ignored otherwise) and `GetRoadPair / SetRoadPair` (only on
  tier 4); both clear when the cell stops being that tier or a road; both reallocate in `Resize`; `Export` / `Import`
  carry them (Import's new parameters default to null = all 0). A static `RoadLayout` helper holds the pure rules:
  `CanStep(grid, from, to)`, `Partner(cell, side)`, `SideRightOf(heading)`, the stretch flood for Reverse.
- **Sim (Simulation asmdef):** `TrafficSystem.Settle` calls `CanStep` when relaxing (the Dial pass runs from the sinks, so
  the relaxation c → nb is the travel step nb → c); `SeedSinks` applies the edge rule. With no directed cell the
  check is two byte reads per edge: flow is unchanged. `RoadNetwork` is not touched. `SaveSystem` (v8): `RoadDirections`,
  `RoadPairs`; `TryFromJson` rejects bytes out of range or on the wrong tier; `SaveMigrations.V7ToV8` = empty layers.
- **Runtime:** `PlacementController` (pair laying, direction, R, demolish pair, hints); `RoadTilemapView` (median
  between paired lanes: the pair side is drawn as a median curb strip instead of a connection); a new
  `RoadArrowView : GridTilemapView` on a `Grid/RoadArrows` tilemap above the roads (4 runtime arrow tiles, painted
  on one-way cells only, so the art set is untouched); `VehicleView` obeys `CanStep` and keeps its right-hand lane;
  `SelectionPanel` (road lines: "Pair of 2", "One-way north", Reverse / Make two-way buttons, the commute "no route");
  `ToolbarController` tooltips (Avenue "2 tiles wide — [R] flip side", Highway "one-way — [R] two-way").

## Risks
- **Per-cell painting needs an axis.** A single click has no direction: the pair falls back to the last drag axis
  (default +x) and the highway to two-way. If that reads badly in the play-through, switch both tools to a
  press-drag-release line with a preview (a bigger change, so only if needed).
- **Directed Dial's cost.** Two byte reads per relaxation; measure in the player, not the Editor's Mono.
- **Pair integrity.** Anything that edits one lane (demolish, fire / rubble on a road cell, upgrade, save edit) must
  leave no dangling `RoadPairs` byte: the setters clear it, `Import` drops a pair whose partner does not point back.
- **Highway networks that strand a home.** Direction mistakes can leave homes with no route; the "no route" line and
  the Traffic view (a zero-flow road stays green) are the player's tools. No auto-fix in 1.1.
