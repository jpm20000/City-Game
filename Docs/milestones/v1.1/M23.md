# M23 — Density & Modern skyline (plan, 2026-10-05)

*Part of the 1.1 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md) (order and shared decisions). Where it fits:
`../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13 (Grid, Simulation, Ages, Land value, Traffic, Save / load, UI).*

**Save:** v9.

## Goal
Three feedback items: the player **zones Low, Medium or High density** for residential, commercial and industrial;
Modern **Medium** level-3 homes and shops stop looking like towers; and the Modern age gets real **Skyscrapers**
(High residential) and **Office blocks** (High commercial). Density is opt-in: a city that never leaves Medium plays
exactly as in 1.0 / 1.1-M22.

## Done when
- A Low / Medium / High selector sits beside the zone buttons; painting stores the density per zoned cell; High is
  hidden (or greyed with the age named) until its age; `SelectionPanel` shows the density and a Density info view
  colours it.
- Capacity, traffic, land value, pollution and the water / power draw follow density through `CapacityModel` and a few
  `BalanceConfig` tunables. **Medium is the identity:** the seeded city and `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline`
  keep every number; `AgeBalanceTests` pass with the engaged player on Medium and with it painting a density mix.
- The sim never changes a cell's density. Repainting changes it; repainting a built cell lower (or higher) keeps
  its level and the sim re-fits residents / jobs to the new capacity, with a confirmation when that would evict.
- Modern Medium L3 R / C read differently from towers; Modern High R = Skyscrapers, High C = Office blocks, with
  Low / High variants for the other ages (placeholder blocks at minimum).
- Save v9 (`Densities`) with a migration step and test; the v1 fixture still migrates; save → load → continue =
  uninterrupted run with a mixed-density city.
- `PerfBenchmark` re-measured (taller meshes, more variants); UI-only play-through passes; docs updated.

## Design decisions (defaults the user can change)
| Topic | Default | Why / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Density enum | `Density : byte { Medium = 0, Low = 1, High = 2 }`: **zero is Medium** | A zero-filled layer *is* today's city, so allocation, `Resize` and the v8 → v9 migration are "empty layer" and nothing has to be back-filled. |
| Where it lives | Per **zoned** cell in `GridData` (`byte[] m_Densities`, `GetDensity / SetDensity`), cleared (→ Medium) when the zone is cleared or the zone type changes; survives `Resize` by reallocation like the other layers | Density belongs to the zone the player painted (Roadmap decision), not to the building: a cell that is demolished and regrows keeps its density. |
| Painting | The zone brush carries a density (`PlacementController.DensityBrush`, default Medium, remembered per session). Painting the same zone with another density *changes the density*; erasing zoning clears it. Seeded / migrated zones are Medium | Same drag code, one more byte. |
| Sim never changes it | Growth moves a cell through levels 1–3 *inside* its density; land value, age, demand never promote or demote it | Roadmap decision (hard ceiling chosen by the player). |
| Capacity | `CapacityModel.Capacity(level, builtAge, density)` = existing capacity × `DensityScale[density]` (rounded, at least 1). Starting numbers: Low 0.5×, High 2.0× for R and C, High 1.6× for I; tuned in 23b/23e like every M1x tunable and tagged "(tuned, M23)" | One scalar keeps `CapacityModel` the only place capacity is computed (standing rule) and Medium = ×1 the identity. A separate per-level table per density is a content change with no gameplay gain. |
| Other effects | Low: land value **+** (`LowDensityLandValue`), fewer trips (follows capacity, so automatically). High: more trips and power / water draw (follow capacity), **industrial pollution × `HighDensityPollution`**, land value neighbours slightly down. All tunables in `BalanceConfig`; the first three come free from capacity | Matches the Roadmap table ("Low: +land value, −traffic. High: more traffic and pollution"). |
| High needs utilities | A **High** cell does not start growing (0 → 1) or level up without power **and** water once those techs are unlocked (the `GameManager.PowerUnlocked` pattern); new `GrowthBlocker.HighDensityNeedsUtilities`, explained in `SelectionPanel` | The Roadmap says High "needs water + power". Medium and Low keep today's gates, so nothing existing changes. |
| High unlock | By age index, in `BalanceConfig`: Commercial from **Renaissance**, Residential and Industrial from **Industrial**; the High button is hidden before that (greyed with "Unlocks in the Industrial age" would crowd the toolbar). Low and Medium are always there. The *Skyscraper / Office block* art appears for High cells **built in Modern** (earlier High cells use the High variant of their own age) | Outline's gating, expressed as data rather than a new `AgeDefinition` field (no age asset regeneration). Art follows the built age, as every visual does. |
| Repainting a built cell | The building keeps its level; capacity is re-fit through the usual residents / jobs update. If the new capacity is below current residents / jobs, `PlacementController` asks first ("N residents would move out") | Cheaper and less destructive than demolishing; "redevelops" in the outline reads as the art swapping to the new band. If the user prefers a real rebuild (level back to 1), it is one line in 23c. |
| Footprint | **Single-cell** for every density; High is taller and wider-body, not multi-cell | Occupancy, growth and selection are per cell. Multi-cell skyscrapers would be a footprint system across all of them; the outline marks it optional. |
| Art | `AgeVisualSet` gets Low / High style tables beside the Medium ones (Medium = the existing arrays, untouched); missing entries fall back to the Medium style scaled by a `GrowthVisuals` profile multiplier (Low 0.6× height, High 1.5×), so every age works before any prefab exists. Modern kit: redesigned Medium L3 R (stepped slab block, warm brick) and C (shops under a low glass band), new High R **Skyscraper** (tall slender glass tower) and High C **Office block** (flat-topped glass box), generated by the kit generator | Fallback keeps the feature complete without art; the generated kit follows the "edit the generator tables, re-run" rule. |
| Density view | A new `InfoOverlay.View.Density` (`IsAvailable` once any zone exists) with Low / Medium / High colours, in the Views flyout | Standing UI pattern; cheaper than adding density hatching to the Zones view. |

## Architecture
- **Pure (Grid asmdef):** `Density` enum; `GridData.m_Densities` + accessors (`SetDensity` ignored on unzoned cells),
  cleared in `SetZone` when the zone changes; `Export` / `Import` carry it (new parameter defaults to null = all
  Medium). `SaveData` v9 `Densities`; `TryFromJson` rejects values > 2 or on unzoned cells; `SaveMigrations.V8ToV9` =
  empty layer.
- **Sim (Simulation asmdef):** `BalanceConfig` tunables (`DensityCapacityScale` per density and zone, `HighDensityMinAge`
  per zone, `LowDensityLandValue`, `HighDensityPollution`); `CapacityModel` density overload (the old signatures stay,
  meaning Medium, so untouched callers and tests compile); `CapacityOf(grid, cell)` reads the cell's density, so
  every caller (population, traffic, power, water, crime, civic) follows with no edits; `GrowthSystem` utilities gate and
  blocker; `LandValueSystem` / `PollutionSystem` read density. Re-fit residents / jobs when `SetDensity` changes a built
  cell (population already re-reads capacity per tick; verify).
- **Runtime:** `PlacementController` (density brush, repaint, eviction confirmation), toolbar density selector
  (`ToolbarController`, hidden High per age), `SelectionPanel` (density line, High blocker text), `InfoOverlay` Density
  view, `GrowthVisuals` / `AgeVisualSet` (variants + profile multipliers), kit generator + Modern prefabs, `EngagedCity`
  (UseDensity option), `PerfBenchmark` (mixed densities).

## Risks
- **Hidden capacity callers.** If any code uses `Capacity(level, age)` or `m_LevelCapacity` directly instead of
  `CapacityOf`, a High cell would be under-counted there. 23b greps for them and the density tests assert totals
  (residents, power, water, trips) against `CapacityOf`.
- **Toolbar room.** The toolbar is full at 1920 (M13 lesson); the density selector must fit inside the zone group
  (three small toggles), checked in a screenshot at 1920 and 1280.
- **Balance.** High ×2 capacity is a lot of free population in one cell; the utilities gate, pollution and traffic are
  the counterweights and 23e measures whether High is a sidegrade or a strictly better choice (probe table, M15 lesson).
- **Perf.** Skyscrapers are taller and more variants: shared materials only, pooled (standing rule).
