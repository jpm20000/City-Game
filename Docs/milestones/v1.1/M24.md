# M24 — Goods (outline, 2026-10-05)

*Outline only: expanded into a full plan (goal, done-when, design decisions, lettered steps, risks) when the
milestone starts (`milestone-workflow`). Part of the 1.1 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md) (order and shared
decisions). Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13.*

**Save:** v10.

- **Sim.** Industry produces goods per worker (per age, Industrial techs raise it); commercial sells goods (its
  jobs and income need supply); homes need a commercial cell within reach that has stock, or lose happiness and
  can't reach the top level. City-wide pool first (simplest, testable), with a reach / traffic term only if the
  pool alone feels flat. A deficit imports goods at a price (Budget ledger line); a surplus exports for income.
- **Identity:** with goods content absent (the age-less sim), supply = demand, so the legacy tests keep their
  numbers. With the shipped content the Industrial baseline changes; the M24 plan states the new numbers and why
  (M12 precedent).
- **UI:** goods meter in the HUD (under demand), Goods view (produce / consume per cell), ledger lines,
  `GrowthBlocker.NoGoods`, happiness tooltip line. Tutorial text if Medieval is affected (it should not be: goods
  could start in the Renaissance, decision in the plan).
- `EngagedCity` balances industry vs commercial; balance tests with goods on, three seeds.
