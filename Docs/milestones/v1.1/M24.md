# M24 — Goods (plan, 2026-10-05)

*Part of the 1.1 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md) (order and shared decisions). Where it fits:
`../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13 (Simulation, Ages & research, Budget, Traffic, Save / load, UI).*

**Save:** v10.

## Goal
One new resource, **goods**: industry makes it, commercial sells it, homes need shops that have stock. A city with
too few factories sees its shops starve and its homes stall below the top level; a city with too many exports the
surplus. It is the only 1.1 milestone that changes the real-content baseline, and it must stay the identity
wherever goods are not in play (the age-less sim, Medieval, Renaissance, the tutorial city).

## Done when
- A city-wide goods pool: industry produces, commercial sells, a stock buffers the difference, a deficit is imported
  at a price (up to a cap) and a surplus above the stock cap is exported for income; both are ledger lines.
- Shortage has teeth: shops earn and employ less, homes lose happiness and cannot reach level 3 while the city is
  short (`GrowthBlocker.NoGoods`, explained in `SelectionPanel` and the happiness tooltip).
- HUD goods meter under demand, a Goods info view (produce / consume per cell), budget ledger lines, tutorial text
  untouched (goods start after the tutorial's ages).
- **Identity:** without ages, and in Medieval and Renaissance, goods are off and every recorded number holds (the
  legacy seeded-city tests, the Renaissance / Medieval age tests). `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` is
  re-recorded with the new numbers and the reasons written here (M12 precedent).
- Save v10 (`GoodsStock`) with a migration step and test; the v1 fixture still migrates; save → load → continue
  equals an uninterrupted run with goods on.
- `EngagedCity` builds and balances factories vs shops; `AgeBalanceTests` with goods on, three seeds, age timings
  within their bands; `PerfBenchmark` unchanged within noise; a UI-only play-through passes; docs updated.

## Design decisions (defaults the user can change)
| Topic | Default | Why / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Starts | **Industrial age** (data: `BalanceConfig.GoodsMinAge` = 2; before it the rule is off). Medieval and Renaissance never see goods. | The Industrial age is where factories and mass production live; it keeps the tutorial and the early ages exactly as shipped. Renaissance start is a one-number change if wanted. Same data-not-asset approach as M23's `HighDensityMinAge` (no age asset regeneration). |
| Model | **City-wide pool**, no reach term in 24a–24d. A cell-level reach (shop within N road cells of a home) only if 24e shows the pool alone is flat. | Simplest and testable; reach drags in the road graph and traffic. The outline's own order. |
| Production | `GoodsPerIndustrialJob` (0.5) × industrial jobs filled (`IndustrialJobs × Employed / Jobs`, same fill rule research uses) × `TechModifiers.GoodsMultiplier`. New tech effect `GoodsMultiplier`; Industrial techs (Factories ×1.25, a later Industrial tech ×1.25) raise it. | Per worker as the outline says; the fill share ties output to the workforce rather than to empty buildings. |
| Demand | Shops: `GoodsPerCommercialJob` (0.35) × commercial jobs. Homes: `GoodsPerResident` (0.05) × population, drawn at the same pool. | Starting numbers tuned in 24d so a seeded city at the usual 0.30 commercial / 0.40 industrial job ratios is roughly balanced (supply ≈ demand) when the player builds a normal mix. |
| Stock | One float: the unsold stock, capped at `GoodsStockDays` (10) × daily demand. Each day: available = stock + production; sell up to demand; remainder refills the stock; above the cap it is exported. | Gives the player a buffer so one fire does not blank the shops, and is the one saved value (v10). |
| Imports | Deficit after the stock is bought at `GoodsImportPrice` (3.0 / unit) up to `GoodsMaxImportShare` (0.5) of daily demand; the rest is a real shortage. Expense line "Imports". | Imports bridge a short gap but cost more than a factory's output earns, so building industry beats living on imports; the cap keeps shortage possible. |
| Exports | Surplus above the stock cap is sold at `GoodsExportPrice` (1.5 / unit). Income line "Exports". | A reward for overbuilding that is smaller than the cost of the jobs behind it; probed in 24d (M15 probe-table lesson). |
| Shortage effect | `Supply = delivered / demand` (1 when covered or imported). Shops: income and research from commercial jobs scale by `GoodsShopFloor (0.4) + (1 − floor) × Supply`. Homes: happiness `−GoodsPenalty (0.08) × cityWeight × (1 − Supply)` (new `HappinessBreakdown.Goods`); level 2 → 3 of a home or shop is blocked while `Supply < GoodsLevel3Supply (0.7)` (`GrowthBlocker.NoGoods`). | Matches the outline (jobs and income need supply; homes lose happiness and can't reach the top level) with data-driven numbers. Level 1–2 never blocked, so a city is never locked at zero. |
| Identity | `AgeRules.Goods` (false in `Legacy`; set from `GoodsMinAge`). Off ⇒ `GoodsSystem` reports Supply = 1, no imports / exports, no stock, no penalty, no growth gate. | Standing rule: the age-less sim is the regression baseline. |
| Roads / traffic | Unchanged: goods are not trips. | Keeps M22's directed graph out of this milestone. |
| Pre-existing cities | Loading a v9 save in the Industrial age or later starts goods with stock 0 and the first day's numbers; the loader does not back-fill. A saved v9 city may therefore see a one-time shortage; acceptable (stock fills in days, imports cover the gap). | Alternative: start with a full stock; no, a v9 → v10 migration should be "empty field". |

## Architecture
- **Pure (Simulation asmdef):** `GoodsSystem` (stock, `Step(production, demand, rules)`, last-day `GoodsReport`:
  produced, demanded, delivered, imported, exported, supply); `BalanceConfig` tunables tagged "(tuned, M24)";
  `AgeRules.Goods`; `TechEffectType.GoodsMultiplier` folded into `TechModifiers`; `BudgetBreakdown` gets `Imports` /
  `Exports` (new optional constructor parameters, 0 default) and `SimulationSystem.Ledger` fills them;
  `HappinessBreakdown.Goods`; `GrowthSystem` gate + `GrowthBlocker.NoGoods`; commercial income and research read the
  shop floor term. Tick order: Demand → Growth → Population → **Goods** → Economy → Research. A `Restore` path
  recomputes the report the same way a tick would.
- **Content (`Tools/gen_age_content.py`):** the `GoodsMultiplier` effect on Factories and a second Industrial tech;
  tech descriptions mention goods. `ContentTests` stays green.
- **Save:** `SaveData.GoodsStock`, `SaveMigrations.V9ToV10` (stock 0), validation (finite, ≥ 0).
- **Runtime / UI:** `GameManager` feeds nothing (derived from the grid and population); `HudController` goods meter
  inside the demand group (hidden while goods are off, the `PowerUnlocked` pattern); `InfoOverlay.View.Goods`
  (produce green / consume amber per cell, always derived from `CapacityOf` shares), `BudgetPanel` ledger lines,
  `SelectionPanel` blocker text and per-cell lines, happiness tooltip line. Tutorial untouched.
- **Harness:** `SeededCity` mirrors the new `BalanceConfig` fields; `EngagedCity` gets a goods rule: when supply
  < 0.9 build industrial (even if industrial demand is low), when exports > 25% of production stop building it.

## Risks
- **Baseline shift.** Every real-content Industrial / Modern balance number moves; the plan accepts it but 24d must
  keep each age's engaged timing within its band by tuning goods numbers, not by loosening asserts.
- **Harness stalls.** A must-build the engaged player doesn't build stalls an age (M13 lesson): the factory rule in
  `EngagedCity` is part of 24d, traced day by day if an age stalls.
- **Death spiral.** Shortage → shop income down → less money for factories. The shop floor, the import cap and
  level 1–2 never being gated are the brakes; 24d probes a city that starts with zero industry.
- **Save compatibility.** One new float; v9 saves load with stock 0.
- **Toolbar / HUD room.** The meter lives inside the demand group; checked in a screenshot at 1920 and 1280.
