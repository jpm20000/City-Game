# M18 — Art & atmosphere (plan, 2026-10-04; **done 2026-10-04**)

*Live plan: update step notes here as steps are done. Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12 (stub + status); systems as built: §13.*

**Goal:** the city looks and sounds like the age it is in. Grown blocks become real little buildings: per age,
zone and level, two or three variants each, facing their street. The art is a **generated kit** (an Editor
generator builds the prefabs from boxes, gables and towers on one shared palette material). It meets the M11
prefab contract, so any slot can later take a hand-made prefab with no code change. Roads and ground get textured
tiles per tier and per age. They are generated PNG sheets that can also be replaced. A **day/night cycle** runs
once per game month: sun angle and colour, a darker ground and roads, and windows that light up at night. Each age
gets **ambience** (birds and wind and bells in the Medieval age, machinery in the Industrial age, traffic hum in the
Modern age), **sound effects** for building, demolishing and the main UI moments, and **music slots** per age that
play the player's tracks once added. Last, **cosmetic vehicles** (carts, carriages, trams, cars) drive on busy
roads, as many as `TrafficSystem` says the road carries. All of it is presentation only. The sim, saves and
balance do not change.

**Done when:**
- Nothing in `CityBuilder.Simulation` changes behaviour. All 336+ EditMode tests stay green with unchanged numbers:
  seeded 24² city, `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` 220 / 0.631, `AgeBalanceTests` off and on. No save version
  bump (`SaveData.CurrentVersion` stays 6).
- **Building kit:** every `AgeVisualSet` slot (4 ages × R/C/I × levels 1–3) has 2–3 kit prefab variants. A
  ContentTests check proves every prefab meets the contract: pivot at the ground centre, inside the 1×1 cell
  (with a small margin), layer 9, a `BoxCollider` on the root, **one renderer and one shared material**
  (`Art/Kit/Kit.mat`), and height within the level's band. Prefabs face a neighbouring road (deterministic). A
  city without ages (no visual set) still draws the placeholder blocks.
- `Tools/gen_age_content.py` keeps the `Prefabs` lists when it rewrites the visual sets. Python owns tints, roofs
  and heights; Unity owns the prefab slots. Re-running the generator leaves no diff.
- **Roads and ground:** a `TileArtSet` asset holds a 16-sprite sheet per road tier and 4 ground variants per age
  (picked per cell by hash). The sheets are generated PNGs (`Tools/gen_tiles.py`). The runtime-drawn road sprites
  stay the fallback when a sheet is missing.
- **Day/night:** one cycle per game month (30 s at 1×), frozen while paused and derived from the calendar, so a
  load shows the right time of day and nothing is saved. The sun, ambient light, ground / road / pipe tilemaps and
  window emission follow it. Info views and the zone overlay always show in daylight. A **Lock to day** setting
  turns it off.
- **Audio:** `Tools/gen_audio.py` (stdlib only, deterministic) synthesizes the SFX and the ambience loops as WAVs.
  An `AudioCatalog` asset lists them, and an `AudioController` plays them on four channels (master / music /
  ambience / SFX) with volumes in `PlayerPrefs`. Ambience crossfades on `AgeChanged` and mixes layers by zoom,
  population and industry. Per-age music slots ship **empty** and play the player's tracks when filled. A
  runtime-built **Sound** panel (volumes, mute, Lock to day) opens from a HUD button.
- **Vehicles:** pooled per-age vehicles (cart → carriage → tram → car, kit meshes on `Kit.mat`) drive along roads
  in the camera view. Their density follows `TrafficSystem.Load / Capacity`. Total vehicles are capped, they freeze
  while paused, they never touch `SimRandom`, and the info views hide them.
- **Perf:** `PerfBenchmark` in a development player stays within budget of the M17f baseline: average frame
  ≤ 1.7 ms (was 1.35), 99th percentile ≤ 2.1 ms (was 1.64), zoomed out ≤ 3.0 ms (was 2.2–2.5), with the kit,
  day/night and vehicles on. A UI-only virtual-input play-through passes, and there are day and night screenshots
  for each age.

## Design decisions (defaults — change any before the step that uses them)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Building art source | **Asked 2026-10-04: generated kit now, your art later.** `BuildingKitGenerator` (Editor) builds every slot from primitives under the prefab contract. A hand-made prefab replaces a slot by editing the `AgeVisualSet` in the Inspector. The contract test checks it too, so a bad import fails ContentTests rather than the frame rate. |
| Audio source | **Asked 2026-10-04: generated SFX and ambience, empty music slots.** `Tools/gen_audio.py` synthesizes all SFX and ambience. Each age's `MusicSet` ships with no tracks. The music player is silent until you add tracks, then plays them with gaps and crossfades. |
| Day/night pacing | **Asked 2026-10-04: one cycle per game month.** Phase = `(day − 1 + partial day) / DaysPerMonth`, from `TimeManager` (new `DayFraction`). 30 s at 1×, 7.5 s at 4×, frozen while paused. Cosmetic only: the sim never reads it. |
| Vehicles | **Asked 2026-10-04: yes, as the last step (18f).** Cut it if 18f blows the perf budget or the step runs long. Nothing earlier depends on it. |
| Who owns `AgeVisualSet` fields | Python (`gen_age_content.py`) owns `Tint`, `Roof`, `RoofColor` and `HeightScale` (still the fallback blocks, and the age-less look). Unity owns `Prefabs`. The generator reads the existing asset and copies each style's `Prefabs:` block through (by age / zone / level). ContentTests asserts the slots are filled, so a regression that wipes them fails. |
| One material | All kit art (buildings and vehicles) uses **one** shared material `Kit.mat` (URP Simple Lit, base map `KitPalette.png`, 16×16 colour swatches at 4 px each, plus an emission map that is black except the window swatches). Faces get UVs at the centre of a swatch, so there is no texture bleed and no mipmap seams (point filter, no mips). One mesh per prefab (combined at generation, one submesh). Draw calls batch well, and info views keep the existing swap-to-tinted-material path, which is now one swap per prefab. |
| Zone legibility | With the zone-coloured blocks gone, zones must still read at a glance. Every kit building carries its **zone accent** on a large surface: residential warm roofs (thatch / terracotta / slate / red), commercial blue-teal awnings and signs, industrial grey sheds with chimneys / saw-tooth roofs. The Zones overlay and info views are unchanged. The 18b screenshots get a "can you tell R / C / I apart at default zoom" check. |
| Facing | `CellUtils.FacingRoad(grid, cell, hash)` (Grid asmdef, pure): the first road neighbour in a fixed order starting from the hash's pick, so corner lots vary. With no road neighbour (a cell that lost its road), the hash picks. `GrowthVisuals` re-faces a grown cell when a 4-neighbour's road changes (`OnCellChanged` on the road cell → resync the 4 neighbours' rotation only). Placeholder blocks are symmetric and ignore it. |
| Kit content per age | See *Content*. Medieval: timber-frame cottages, thatch / wooden-shingle gables, small; Renaissance: rendered townhouses, terracotta, loggias; Industrial: brick terraces, slate, factory sheds, chimneys; Modern: concrete / glass slabs, flat roofs, towers. Heights follow today's `Profile` bands × the age's `HeightScale`, so skylines read the same as the placeholders. |
| Ground and roads per age | **Roads look by tier, not by age** (the tiers already are the ages' roads: dirt and cobble are early, avenue and highway modern). The **ground** changes by the city's current age: Medieval meadow (long grass, wildflowers), Renaissance tended grass, Industrial sooty grass, Modern lawn. There are 4 variants each, picked by cell hash. Repainted on `AgeChanged` / `CityLoaded` / `WorldResized` (one `SetTiles` call, like `PaintGround`). |
| Night look | Sun: elevation follows the phase (noon 50°, dusk/dawn near the horizon, night = a dim blue "moon" at 35°). Colour and intensity come from `Gradient` / `AnimationCurve` fields on `DayNightCycle` (day 1.1 white → dusk 0.7 orange → night 0.25 blue). Ambient follows `RenderSettings.ambientLight`. **Sprite-unlit tilemaps ignore lights**, so `Tilemap.color` on Ground / Roads / Pipes / Hazards gets the same night tint (floor 0.45 so the city stays readable). Windows: `Kit.mat` `_EmissionColor` × night factor (one shared material, set once a frame only when the value changes). Per age, window light is warm candle (Medieval / Renaissance) or bright (Industrial / Modern). Info views, the zone overlay and the placement ghost force **day**, smoothly over 0.3 s. No post-processing (bloom) in M18. It is a candidate if the night looks flat. |
| No save, settings in PlayerPrefs | Day/night is derived from the date. Volumes, mute and Lock to day are player settings in `PlayerPrefs` (`CityGame.Audio.*`, `CityGame.Visual.LockDay`), not city data, so there is no `SaveData` change. M19's settings menu will move them into its own screen. |
| Audio model | No `AudioMixer` asset (Unity has no script API to build one, and hand-written mixer YAML is fragile). The channel volume is applied in code to each channel's `AudioSource`s. All sounds are 2D (the iso camera has no meaningful 3D position). Building SFX are panned by the screen x of the cell (±0.6) and quieter when zoomed out. One-shots come from a pool of 12 sources, each SFX entry has 1–3 clips with ±6% pitch jitter, and the same SFX is rate-limited to once per 60 ms, so a road drag doesn't machine-gun. |
| Ambience mix | Per age, three looping layers: **base** (nature / wind; louder when zoomed in and in small towns), **city** (crowd / carts / traffic by age; volume by population and grown cells in view), **industry** (workshops / mills / factories; volume by the share of industrial cells in view). The camera view rectangle is sampled every 0.5 s (counts from `GridData` in the view, not a full-map scan). Crossfade 3 s on `AgeChanged`. Fires on screen add a crackle loop by the burning count. At night the base layer shifts to a night variant (crickets) and the city layer drops 40%. |
| Hooks for SFX | Runtime only, no sim change. `PlacementController` (place, zone paint, road lay / upgrade, pipe, demolish, refused / no money), `ToolButton` (click), `NotificationController` (toast ping), `TechPanel` / `GameEvents.TechCompleted` (research complete), `AgeChanged` (age fanfare), `EventPopup` (open, choice), `DisasterController` (fire starts, plague bell, breakdown clank), `GrowthVisuals` (a soft "pop" for level-ups in view, rate-limited to one per 0.25 s). They call `AudioController.Play(SfxId, worldPos?)`, a static that is a no-op when no controller exists (EditMode tests, the harness). |
| Vehicles model | Purely visual, in `VehicleView` (runtime). Every 0.5 s it lists the road cells in the camera view. The target count = Σ `load / capacity × VehiclesPerFullRoad (1.5)` over those cells, capped at `MaxVehicles` (250). It spawns at load-weighted cells using `System.Random` (its own, never `SimRandom`, never the sim). Each vehicle drives cell to cell along roads and picks the next cell weighted by load. Speed is the tier's (`1 / TravelCost` scaled), × game speed, and stops while paused. A vehicle is released when it leaves the view or reaches a dead end or the map edge. One kit mesh per age's vehicle on `Kit.mat`, one renderer, no collider, layer 0 (not selectable). It drives on the right of the cell centre line, rotated to the travel direction. Headlights are the window swatch, so they glow at night. Hidden while an info view is on. |
| Placed buildings | Services and utilities keep their M9–M14 primitive prefabs in M18 (they are already per-age and readable). Re-skinning them onto `Kit.mat` (fewer renderers) is a candidate after the benchmark, not a commitment. |
| Fallbacks | No `AgeVisualSet` / empty slot → placeholder blocks (today). No `TileArtSet` / missing sheet → runtime-drawn roads and the plain ground tile. No `AudioCatalog` → silence, no errors. The game is playable with any of these missing. |

## Content (first pass; looks are tunables in the generator tables)

**Building kit** (`BuildingKitGenerator`'s tables; 3 variants per slot except where noted; footprint % of a cell,
height in cells = today's `Profile` × the age's `HeightScale`):

| Age | Residential L1 / L2 / L3 | Commercial L1 / L2 / L3 | Industrial L1 / L2 / L3 | Palette |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medieval | wattle cottage, thatched gable · two-storey timber house, jettied upper floor · timber row house with dormers | market stall + awning · shop-house with sign · guild hall with tower | smithy (open lean-to, chimney) · tannery / workshop yard · watermill-style shed | timber, daub cream, thatch gold, dark wood |
| Renaissance | rendered house, terracotta gable · townhouse with balcony · palazzo block, loggia | shop with arcade · merchant house, sign · bank / exchange with cornice | brick workshop · warehouse with hoist · kiln with conical chimney | sandstone, ochre render, terracotta |
| Industrial | brick cottage, slate · brick terrace (2–3 houses in a cell) · tenement block | corner shop, blue awning · department store · office block with clock | factory shed, saw-tooth roof · mill with tall stack · steelworks hall + two stacks | red brick, slate, soot grey, iron |
| Modern | bungalow, flat roof + carport · low-rise flats · residential tower | glass-front shop · office slab · glass tower with crown | warehouse box · plant with tanks · clean-tech hall with rooftop solar | concrete, glass teal, white, steel |

Vehicles: Medieval handcart / ox cart, Renaissance horse carriage, Industrial horse tram and early truck, Modern car,
bus and van. Two or three per age, one picked by hash at spawn.

**Ground** (`gen_tiles.py`, 64 px tiles matching `ground_square`, 4 variants per age): meadow with flowers
(Medieval), tended grass (Renaissance), sooty grass with paths (Industrial), mown lawn (Modern).
**Road sheets**: the five tiers' looks from M16 (dirt, cobble, paved, avenue, highway) redrawn with texture
(ruts, stone pattern, asphalt grain, worn markings) as 4×4 sheets in the M16 connection-mask order.

**SFX** (`SfxId`, append-only enum): `Click`, `Place`, `ZonePaint`, `RoadLay`, `RoadUpgrade`, `PipeLay`, `Demolish`,
`Refused`, `NoMoney`, `Toast`, `LevelUp`, `ResearchDone`, `AgeAdvance`, `EventOpen`, `EventChoice`, `FireStart`,
`PlagueBell`, `Breakdown`, `Repair`, `Save`, `Load`.

**Ambience loops** (20–30 s each, mono 22.05 kHz, Vorbis in the import settings): per age base / city / industry,
plus shared `night`, `fire`. That is 14 loops. Generated with seamless loop crossfades.

## Architecture

**Pure (Grid / Simulation asmdefs; small, testable):**

| New / changed | Notes |
|---|---|
| `CellUtils.FacingRoad(grid, cell, hash)` (Grid) | Rotation 0..3 toward a road neighbour (see Facing). Tests in `CellUtilsTests`. |
| `CalendarMath` (Simulation, new static) | `DayPhase(day, dayFraction, daysPerCycle)` → 0..1, `NightFactor(phase)` (0 day … 1 midnight, smooth dusk / dawn). Tests: phase wraps at month end, monotonic, dusk symmetric. |
| `TrafficSystem` | Unchanged API. `VehicleView` reads `Load` / `Capacity` and the `GridData` road tier. |

**Runtime (`Assembly-CSharp`):**

| New / changed | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Scripts/Editor/BuildingKitGenerator.cs` | Menu *CityBuilder → Generate Building Kit*. Builds `Art/Kit/KitPalette.png` + `KitEmission.png`, `Kit.mat`, combined meshes under `Art/Kit/Meshes/`, prefabs under `Prefabs/Kit/<Age>/` (`R1a`, `R1b`, …) and vehicle meshes. Assigns the prefabs into the 4 `AgeVisualSet`s via `SerializedObject`. It is deterministic (seeded per slot), and a re-run rewrites the same assets in place (keeps GUIDs). Uses the `scene-prefab-editing` asset rules. |
| `Scripts/Editor/ArtContract.cs` | `Validate(GameObject prefab, int level, List<string> errors)`: pivot / bounds / layer / root collider / renderer + material count / height band. Menu *Validate Art* for hand-made imports. ContentTests reaches it by reflection like the visual-set check. |
| `AgeVisualSet` | No new fields. The contract comment points to `ArtContract`. |
| `GrowthVisuals` | Prefab rotation from `FacingRoad`, neighbour re-facing, level-up sound hook. Prefab pooling and the tint path are unchanged. |
| `TileArtSet` (SO, `Scripts/Placement/`) | `Sprite[] RoadSheets` per tier (16 each, mask order), `Sprite[] Ground` per age (4 each). `RoadTilemapView` uses a sheet when present, otherwise draws as today. `GridSystem.PaintGround` picks the variant per cell by hash for the current age. |
| `DayNightCycle` (`Scripts/Core/`) | Reads `TimeManager.DayFraction`, `CalendarMath`, the age (window colour), `InfoOverlay` / ghost state (force day). Drives the light, ambient, tilemap colours, `Kit.mat` emission and the camera background. `LockDay` setting. |
| `TimeManager` | `+ DayFraction` (accumulator / `SecondsPerDay`, 0 when paused mid-day stays put). No behaviour change. |
| `Scripts/Audio/` (new folder): `SfxId`, `AudioCatalog` (SO: SFX entries, per-age `AmbienceSet` {base, city, industry} + `MusicSet` {tracks}, shared night / fire loops), `AudioController` (channels, pool, ambience mixer, music player, static `Play`), `AudioSettings` (PlayerPrefs) | Created at runtime by `GameManager` from one serialized `m_AudioCatalog` reference (the one scene edit for audio). |
| `SoundPanel` (`Scripts/UI/`, runtime-built like `BudgetPanel`) | In the `SidePanels` slot: Master / Music / Ambience / SFX sliders, Mute, Lock to day. It opens from a speaker button added beside Save / Load / New (`GameMenu`), built at runtime, so no HUD prefab edit. |
| `VehicleView` (`Scripts/Placement/`) | See Vehicles model. Created at runtime by `GameManager` with a `VehicleSet` (per-age meshes, made by the kit generator). |
| `PerfBenchmark` | The stress city gets the kit (it already mixes ages) + a night phase + vehicles on a loaded road grid. It reports frames with and without vehicles. |
| `Tools/gen_age_content.py` | Preserves `Prefabs` blocks (see decisions). |
| `Tools/gen_tiles.py`, `Tools/gen_audio.py` (new) | Stdlib only (`zlib` / `struct` PNG writer; `wave` / `math` synth with a fixed seed). Outputs `Art/Tiles/*.png` and `Audio/Generated/*.wav`, with hand-written `.meta`s with deterministic GUIDs (uuid5, like the content generator) and the import settings preset (point filter / no mips for tiles; Vorbis, load in background, streaming for loops). |

## Standing rules check

- **Pure sim / age-less baseline:** no sim behaviour change. The only Simulation addition is `CalendarMath`
  (a pure function nothing in `Tick` calls). Every recorded number stays as it is.
- **Saves:** none. Day/night is derived; settings are player preferences.
- **Per-cell state:** `GrowthVisuals` already rebuilds on `OnResized`. `VehicleView` clears its pool on
  `WorldResized` / `CityLoaded`. Ground variants repaint on resize.
- **Content generator:** visual-set tints stay in `gen_age_content.py`. Prefab slots are Unity's, preserved.
  `ContentTests` extended.
- **Rendering:** one shared kit material, one renderer per building / vehicle, pooled. Emission is a shared-material
  property (no `MaterialPropertyBlock`s). `PerfBenchmark` re-run in 18b, 18f and 18g.
- **UI patterns:** the Sound panel is in `SidePanels`. New events (if any) go in `GameEvents` + `ResetSubscribers`.
  Nothing is tech-gated (the art follows the built age by itself).

## Risks / open questions

- **Generated art can look like programmer art.** The kit targets readable silhouettes with a consistent palette,
  not detail. Your hand-made prefabs are the real fix, slot by slot. If a slot looks worse than the tinted block,
  leave it empty (the block shows).
- **Zone legibility** with real buildings: mitigated by the zone accents; if the 18b screenshots fail the check, add
  a thin zone-coloured ground plate under kit buildings (the zone tile is already drawn; raise its alpha under
  grown cells).
- **Night readability:** the tint floor of 0.45 and force-day in views; Lock to day as the escape hatch.
- **Repo size:** WAVs at 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit: ~1.3 MB per 30 s loop → ~20 MB of loops + small SFX. If that is too
  much, shorten the loops to 20 s or commit 8-bit / lower rates for the base beds. Tiles are tiny. Meshes are binary
  `.asset`s (Force Text serialization makes them YAML; check the size after 18b and keep them under ~5 MB total).
- **Generated `.meta`s for WAV / PNG:** the import settings must be written right the first time, or Unity rewrites
  them and leaves churn. Generate once, let Unity import, commit Unity's version, and have the scripts keep a meta that
  already exists.
- **Python vs Unity both writing `AgeVisualSet`:** mitigated by the ownership rule and the preserve test; the
  kit generator never touches tints, and the Python generator never touches prefabs.
- **Sprite-unlit tilemaps under day/night:** `Tilemap.color` multiplies the tiles' own colours; views that recolour
  tiles must set day colour first (force-day handles it).
- **Audio can't be verified by the MCP bridge.** Checks are "which source played what" logs. A human listen is
  owed and recorded in the 18e note.
- **Vehicles at 96² zoomed out:** the view-limited spawn and the cap bound it; 18f is cuttable.
