# M17 — Disasters & events (plan, 2026-10-04; **done**)

*Live plan — update step notes here as steps are done. Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12 (stub + status), systems as built: §13.*


**Goal:** the city can lose what it built. Three **hazards** grow out of numbers the sim already has. **Fires**
start on blocks with M14 fire risk. They spread block to block, faster through timber ages, and burn down grown
blocks and placed buildings that fire cover doesn't save. **Plague** breaks out in the Medieval and Renaissance
ages where homes lack health cover, spreads between nearby homes and kills residents. **Plant breakdowns** shut
down power plants, water towers and pumping stations for a while; old or underfunded ones break down more often.
On top of that, **random events** with two or three choices arrive every month or two as a popup that pauses the
game (a travelling fair, a poor harvest, a workers' strike, a world's fair...). Each choice has a price and
temporary effects. Randomness comes from one seeded RNG whose state is saved, so save → load → continue still
equals an uninterrupted run. A **Disasters & events** switch in the New City dialog turns all of it off.

**Done when:**
- `SimRandom` (seeded, pure, state saved) is the only source of randomness in the sim. With the switch off, or in
  a sim built without ages, nothing draws from it and every number matches M16 (seeded 24² city,
  `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` 220 / 0.631, `AgeBalanceTests` off-mode 84 / 61 / 57).
- **Fire:** ignitions follow `CivicSystem.GetFireRisk` (so fire cover, built age, industry and the Fire Code /
  Building Code ordinances already count). A fire spreads to 4-neighbour buildings (grown or placed; roads are
  firebreaks, with a small chance to jump a 1-wide street) and is put out by fire cover, less well without water.
  If it burns for `FireBurnDays`, the building is destroyed. A grown block drops to level 0 under rubble
  (`GrowthBlocker.Rubble`) and regrows from its zone afterwards. A placed building is removed with no refund, and
  the runtime (and the harness) drop it from their building lists.
- **Plague** (Medieval 1.0, Renaissance 0.6, other ages 0): outbreaks follow mean sickness, spread to homes in
  reach by their sickness, and kill a share of the residents of infected homes each day. A **Plague**
  happiness term applies while it lasts, and a cooldown follows each outbreak.
- **Breakdowns:** a plant / tower / pump that breaks down gives 0 supply for `BreakdownDays`, or until the player
  repairs it from its panel (20% of its cost). Older tech (by ages behind) and lower funding of its budget line
  make breakdowns more likely; Smart Grid (finally an effect) cuts power breakdowns to a quarter.
- **Events:** 13 tech/age-gated events with 2–3 choices from the content generator. A popup pauses the game
  until the player picks, and an unanswered event takes its free last choice after `EventAutoDays`. Effects are
  instant (money, research points) and/or temporary `TechEffect`s for N days, folded into `TechModifiers`. Their
  happiness goes to a separate **Events** term.
- The player sees all of it: burning cells (pooled flame / smoke blocks), rubble and plague tiles on a hazard
  tilemap, toasts for each hazard, `SelectionPanel` lines (on fire, rubble, plague, broken down + Repair button),
  happiness tooltip lines (Plague, Events), and the event popup. DEBUG buttons start each hazard and offer an
  event (play-through and screenshots).
- Save **v6** stores the switch, the RNG state, fires, rubble, plague, broken sources, the pending event and
  active event effects. v5 and the v1 fixture migrate with **disasters off**. Save → load → continue with a fire
  burning, an outbreak running, a plant broken down and an event effect active equals an uninterrupted run.
- `AgeBalanceTests` hold (each age in 45–90 days from Medieval, never in debt, happiness ≥ 0.55) with disasters
  **off** (unchanged numbers) and **on** for fixed seeds, with `UseBudget` on and off. The engaged player builds
  fire / health cover (it already does), repairs broken plants when it can, rebuilds burnt buildings and answers
  events by a rule. The hazard step costs ≤ 0.15 ms per tick on a full 96² city (EditMode). All EditMode tests
  are green, and a UI-only virtual-input play-through passes.

## Design decisions (defaults — change any before the step that uses them)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Fire damage | **Asked 2026-10-04: blocks and placed buildings burn.** A grown block that burns out drops to level 0 and leaves rubble. A placed building (park, service, plant, well...) burns as a whole footprint: any cell catching fire sets every cell with its occupant id burning, and burning out destroys it with no refund. Roads and pipes never burn. |
| Event UI | **Asked 2026-10-04: a popup that pauses.** Events pause the game until a choice is picked, and the previous speed comes back afterwards. Hazards (fire, plague, breakdown) are toasts only and never pause. |
| Defaults | **Asked 2026-10-04: new cities on, old saves off.** The New City toggle defaults to on (the startup city counts as new: on). `V5ToV6` migrates with `Disasters = false`, so existing cities play as before. `SimulationSystem` defaults to off, so every pure test and the age-less sim are unchanged unless a test turns it on. |
| Balance | **Asked 2026-10-04: both modes, fixed seeds.** `EngagedCity.UseDisasters` (off by default). `AgeBalanceTests` run off (today's numbers, the regression baseline) and on for seeds 1, 2 and 3 (more if a seed turns out to be an outlier), with and without `UseBudget`. |
| One switch | One `Disasters` bool covers fires, plague, breakdowns and events. The plan keeps the four systems separable, so splitting it into two switches later (hazards / events) is a UI and save change only. |
| Age-less sim | Hazards and events need age content: plague risk per age, events in `TechDatabase.Events`. The age-less sim (`Tech == null`) never runs them, even if a test flips the switch: `Disasters.Enabled` is false without ages. The legacy baseline is untouched by construction. |
| RNG | `SimRandom` (Simulation asmdef): xorshift64* with a SplitMix64-seeded state. `NextFloat()` gives [0,1), `NextInt(n)`, and `State` / `Restore(state)` save it as a 16-digit hex string (JsonUtility-safe). It is owned by `DisasterSystem` and drawn only inside `DisasterSystem.Step`, in a fixed order: events → ignition → spread / extinguish → plague → breakdowns. The draw **count** depends only on sim state, never on frame timing or UI. New City seeds it from `Environment.TickCount` (tests pass a seed). The seed itself isn't shown or saved, only the state. |
| When it runs | `DisasterSystem.Step()` runs in `Tick` after research and **before** `UpdateTraffic`, so the traffic flow sees today's losses (and `Restore` recomputes it the same way). Effects land the same day: a cell burnt out today is at level 0 in tonight's capacity; plague deaths go through `Population.LoseResidents(n)` (new; also lowers `Employed` and the rest of the recount on the next `RecountCapacity`). |
| Ignition | Not one draw per cell, which costs a draw per cell and would bias the row-major scan. Each day: `λ = FireIgnitionPerRisk (0.0015) × Σ GetFireRisk(cell)` over grown cells, ignition with probability `1 − e^−λ` (one draw), then a cell picked by risk-weighted choice (one draw, cumulative scan). At most **one new fire a day**. Fire risk is already 0 below `CivicFreePopulation` (the ramp), so a new town never burns. Target (16b probe): an uncovered Medieval town of 300 sees about one fire a month; a fully covered one, one every few months. |
| Spread | Burning cells are processed row-major. Each one tries its 4-neighbours in `Neighbors4` order: a grown or placed cell that isn't burning or rubble catches fire with `p = FireSpreadChance (0.3) × flammability × (1 − fireCover(target)) × FireSpread multiplier`. Flammability = the built age's `FireRisk / BalanceConfig.FireRisk` (Medieval 1.7, Renaissance 1.3, Industrial 1.0, Modern 0.57) × 1.5 for industry. Placed buildings use `PlacedFlammability` (0.5) × the current age's ratio. A road cell between two buildings in a straight line lets the fire **jump** with `p × FireJumpFactor (0.15)`; wider roads stop it. Fires lit today start spreading tomorrow (no chain in one day). |
| Extinguish / burn out | Each day a burning cell is put out with `p = min(0.95, FireExtinguishBase 0.1 + fireCover × FireExtinguishPerCover 0.7) × (no water in a water age ? DryExtinguishFactor 0.5 : 1)`. Put out = the building survives intact. Otherwise `FireDays` counts up, and at `FireBurnDays` (3) it is destroyed. Grown → `SetBuildingLevel(0)` + rubble. Placed → every cell of the occupant becomes rubble, and the occupant id goes into `Disasters.Fire.Destroyed` (cleared each Step). |
| Rubble | Per-cell days left (`RubbleDays` 15), blocks growth (`GrowthBlocker.Rubble`, appended). Placing a road / building or zoning on rubble clears it. The demolish tool clears rubble for free. Saved. |
| Destroyed placed buildings | The sim cannot remove runtime objects. `SimulationSystem.BuildingsDestroyed` (event, occupant ids) fires at the end of the tick. `GameManager` removes each instance through the `PlacementController` demolish path (no refund), which unregisters it and re-sets `Sources` before the next tick. `EngagedCity` and the test helper drop the matching sources the same way. In between, the sim ignores sources whose footprint lost its occupant (a `GridData.GetOccupant` check in `ApplySources`), so a harness that forgets can't keep a ghost plant powered. |
| Plague | `AgeDefinition.PlagueRisk` (generator column: Medieval 1.0, Renaissance 0.6, Industrial / Modern 0; 0 without ages). When no outbreak is running, the cooldown is 0 and population ≥ `PlagueMinPopulation` (120): outbreak with `p = PlagueOutbreakPerDay (0.004) × PlagueRisk × mean sickness` (housing-weighted, `CivicSystem.GetSickness`, so health cover and Herb Gardens count), patient zero by sickness × capacity weighted choice. Spread: each infected home tries every home within `PlagueRadius` (2, Chebyshev) with `p = PlagueSpread (0.12) × sickness(target) × PlagueSpread multiplier`. A home stays infected for `PlagueDays` (10), then recovers and is immune until the outbreak ends (byte 255). When no home is infected, the outbreak ends and the cooldown `PlagueCooldownDays` (180) starts. Deaths per day: residents of infected homes (capacity × occupancy) × `PlagueDeathRate` (0.01), rounded down with the remainder carried (deterministic, no draws). |
| Plague happiness | `HappinessBreakdown.Plague` = −`min(infectedHousingShare × PlaguePenalty 0.5, PlaguePenaltyCap 0.15)` while an outbreak runs (fear is city-wide). Not ramped (outbreaks need 120 residents anyway). |
| Breakdowns | Sources with `PowerSupply > 0` or `WaterSupply > 0` (plant, tower, pump; wells and fountains are coverage and never break). Per day at most one breakdown starts: `λ = Σ BreakdownPerDay (0.003) × (1 + BreakdownPerAgeBehind 0.5 × agesBehind) × 1/f² × Breakdown multiplier`, where f = the funding of its budget line (150% → ×0.44, 50% → ×4) and agesBehind = current age − its tech's age. The source is picked by weighted choice. A broken source is fed to Power / Water with 0 supply for `BreakdownDays` (10). `SimulationSystem.Repair(origin)` spends `RepairCostFraction` (0.2) × its cost and fixes it now. `ServiceSource` gains optional trailing `Cost` and `TechAge` (filled by `GameManager.RebuildSources` and `EngagedCity`; default 0 / current age). Broken sources are keyed by origin cell, saved as `(x, y, daysLeft)`. A save entry with no matching source on load is dropped. |
| New effect type | `TechEffectType.HazardMultiplier` (append only), Target `FireSpread` / `PlagueSpread` / `Breakdown`. Content: **Steel Frames** fire spread ×0.5 ("buildings that don't burn like kindling"; its first effect), **Smart Grid** breakdown ×0.25 (its first effect), **Building Code** ordinance gains fire spread ×0.8, a new Medieval ordinance **Quarantine** (Herbalism, $0.02/resident: plague spread ×0.5, commercial demand ×0.9). Ordinances 12 → 13. |
| Events | `EventDefinition` (Simulation asmdef SO: Id, Title, Text, `MinAge` / `MaxAge` (indices), `MinPopulation`, `RequiredTech` (optional), `Weight`, `Choices[]`) with `EventChoice {Label, Cost, CostPerResident, Reward (money), ResearchPoints, Effects (TechEffect[]), Days}`, listed in **`TechDatabase.Events`** (like ordinances and road tiers; `Validate` checks unique ids, 2–3 choices, last choice free, techs in the database, `MinAge ≤ MaxAge`). `EventSystem`: the first event comes after `EventFirstDays` (30), then every `EventIntervalMin..Max` (45–90) days (one draw), picked by weight among the eligible ones (one draw). Eligible = age in range, population, tech, and not offered in the last `EventRepeatDays` (360). The event is `Pending` until `Choose(i)` (fails if the city can't afford choice i; the last choice is always free). An unanswered event takes the last choice after `EventAutoDays` (10) of ticks. The popup pauses, so only DEBUG skips and the harness ever tick past it. Active effects `{eventId, choice, daysLeft}` are folded into `TechModifiers` after researched techs and enacted ordinances. Their `HappinessBonus` goes to a new `TechModifiers.EventHappiness` → `HappinessBreakdown.Events`. |
| Saves | **v6**, one bump for the whole milestone in 17a (the later steps fill fields that already exist): `Disasters` (bool), `RandomState` (hex string), `Fires` / `Rubble` / `Plague` (row-major bytes: fire days, rubble days left, plague days or 255), `PlagueCooldown`, `PlagueRemainder`, `Broken` (`BrokenRecord {X, Y, DaysLeft}`), `PendingEvent` (id, "" = none) + `PendingDays`, `DaysToNextEvent`, `ActiveEvents` (`EventRecord {Id, Choice, DaysLeft}`), `RecentEvents` (`EventRecord` with `DaysLeft` = days until it may repeat). `V5ToV6`: disasters off, empty layers, zero state. Validation: byte layers sized `Width × Height`, unknown event ids dropped (counted like unknown techs). |
| Toasts | Fire: "Fire! A block is burning — fire cover puts fires out (Fire view)" (first fire per city carries the hint; later ones are short), and "The fire is out: n blocks and the X destroyed". Plague: "Plague has broken out — homes without health care catch it" / "The plague is over: n died". Breakdown: "The X has broken down — repair it ($c) or wait n days". An event's result: "Fair hosted: −$300". The popup itself isn't a toast. |
| Feedback without a new view | Fire risk and sickness already have views (Services → Fire / Health). Burning, rubble and plague are always drawn (a hazard tilemap + flame blocks), not behind a view. A **Hazards** view is a candidate only if the play-through shows they get lost in a big city. |

## Steps (each one fits a session and is committed on its own)

- **17a RNG, switch and save v6 (pure).** `SimRandom`, `DisasterSystem` shell (`Enabled`, `Random`, empty
  `Step`), every v6 field, `V5ToV6`, `CreateNew(..., disasters, seed)`, `Capture` / `ApplySimulation` round trip of
  the switch and RNG state, `AgeDefinition.PlagueRisk` + generator column, `TechEffectType.HazardMultiplier` +
  `TechModifiers` fold (not read yet), `ServiceSource.Cost` / `TechAge`. Runtime: `SaveGameController.NewCity`
  passes `disasters: true` and a tick-count seed (no dialog toggle yet). Tests (new `DisasterTests.cs`): RNG
  determinism and state round trip (same seed → same 1,000 draws; restore mid-stream continues the stream);
  `Enabled` is false without ages; v5 → v6 (disasters off, empty layers); v1 fixture still plays like the pre-ages
  game; layers survive `Resize` (reallocated empty). All existing tests green, with no number moved.
- **17b Fire (pure).** `FireSystem` (ignition, spread, jump, extinguish, burn out, rubble), `GrowthBlocker.Rubble`,
  `BuildingsDestroyed` + the occupant check in `ApplySources`, `HazardMultiplier FireSpread` read, content (Steel
  Frames, Building Code), save fields filled. Tests: no fire below `CivicFreePopulation`; ignition rate matches
  `1 − e^−λ` over many seeded days (statistical bound); risk-weighted pick; spread blocked by a 2-wide road, jumps a
  1-wide one at the jump rate; fire cover puts fires out and lowers spread; dry cells harder to put out; burn out at
  day 3 → level 0 + rubble, regrows after `RubbleDays`; a placed 2×2 burns as one and is reported once; a destroyed
  plant's supply is gone before the runtime removes it; Steel Frames halves spread; save → load → continue with a
  fire burning = uninterrupted run. **Probe** (`[Test, Explicit]`, deleted before commit): fires per 100 days, mean
  blocks lost per fire for the seeded city and `EngagedCity` at each age's end, with and without fire cover, to
  tune `FireIgnitionPerRisk` / `FireSpreadChance` to the targets in the decision table.
- **17c Plague and breakdowns (pure).** `PlagueSystem`, `Population.LoseResidents`, the Plague term,
  `BreakdownSystem` in `ApplySources`, `Repair`, the Breakdown multiplier (Smart Grid), Quarantine ordinance. Tests:
  no plague in Industrial / Modern or below 120 residents; outbreak rate by mean sickness; spread by the target's
  sickness, none to covered homes; immunity and the end + cooldown; deaths deterministic with the carried
  remainder; the Plague term formula and cap; breakdown rate by ages behind, funding and Smart Grid; a broken
  plant feeds 0 and its homes go dark (power growth gate); repair spends 20% and restores it; breakdown entry
  dropped on load when its source is gone; save → load → continue with an outbreak and a broken plant. Probe:
  outbreaks per Medieval / Renaissance age and deaths per outbreak, with and without an Apothecary; breakdowns
  per 100 days per age.
- **17d Events (pure + content).** `EventDefinition` / `EventChoice`, `TechDatabase.Events` + validation,
  `EventSystem` (cadence, eligibility, choose, auto-choice, active effects, recent list), the `TechModifiers` fold
  and the Events happiness term, `EVENTS` in the generator, Quarantine / Steel Frames / Smart Grid descriptions.
  Tests: cadence within the interval; eligibility by age / population / tech / repeat window; `Choose` charges cost
  + per-resident cost, pays rewards and RP, fails when unaffordable (the last choice always works); effects fold
  and expire after `Days`; auto-choice after `EventAutoDays`; save keeps the pending event and active effects and
  drops unknown ids; save → load → continue with an active effect. `ContentTests`: 13 events valid, each age has
  ≥ 3 eligible events, every gating tech exists, 13 ordinances.
- **17e UI.** New City toggle, `EventPopup` (pause / resume), `HazardView`, `FireVisuals`, removal of destroyed
  placed buildings, `SelectionPanel` lines + Repair button, happiness tooltip, toasts, rubble in placement /
  zoning / demolish, DEBUG buttons, `GameEvents`. Play-mode screenshots (`unity-playmode-check`; back up and

- **17f Balance, perf, play-through, docs.** `EngagedCity.UseDisasters`: the player rebuilds burnt placed
  buildings (they come back into its plan the way a missing building would), repairs a broken plant or tower when
  cash covers it above the cushion (else waits), and answers events with a rule: the first choice when its cost

## Risks / open questions

- **Determinism.** Any draw outside `DisasterSystem.Step`, or a draw count that depends on runtime state (e.g. a
  source list order that differs between a fresh run and a load), breaks save → continue. `Sources` order comes
  from `GameManager`'s placed list, which a load rebuilds in save order. Pick breakdowns by sorted origin (row-major),
  not list order, and test with sources added in two different orders.
- **Destroyed buildings cross the pure / runtime line.** The sim reports occupant ids and the runtime removes the
  instances. If the removal path ever refunds, toasts "Demolished" or skips `UnregisterBuilding`, money or sources
  drift. The occupant check in `ApplySources` makes a missed removal harmless for supply, but 17e must test the
  real path, and `EngagedCity` must mirror it.
- **A disaster spiral.** A burnt plant in an Industrial city → no power → no upgrades → unhappy → lower income →
  no money to rebuild. **Decided 2026-10-04: no fallback, the spiral is a purposeful challenge** (Utility
  buildings stay flammable). Plants burn only by spread (they never ignite themselves) and the debt banner already
  offers a loan, so the player's counters are fire cover, loans and spare plants. The balance tests still have to
  pass with the engaged player, which is the check that it stays survivable.
- **Balance noise.** Random runs make `AgeBalanceTests` seed-dependent. Fixed seeds keep them reproducible, but a
  tuning change can push one seed past a bound. Report all three, and widen nothing without asking.
- **One fire a day and a capped spread** keep a 96² city from burning every day. Watch that fire doesn't become
  noise there: per-cell ignition risk is the same, so a big city sees more fires. A per-capita cap may be needed
  (decide from the 17b probe).
- **Pausing popups and the harness.** `EngagedCity` and the sim tests never pause: they answer through `Choose`, or
  the auto-choice applies. DEBUG Skip 30 days with an event pending takes the auto-choice. Note it in the debug
  panel tooltip.
- **Plague deaths vs population growth.** Move-in is `ceil(Vacant × 0.20)`, so a town refills fast. The Plague
  happiness term and the death rate both matter; tune so an uncovered outbreak is felt (a visible dip) without
  ending the Medieval age.
- **Toolbar / HUD space:** none needed (no new buttons, no new view). The popup and the New City toggle are runtime UI.
- **Not in M17 (later candidates):** floods, earthquakes, riots from crime, insurance or a disaster fund, a
  Hazards info view, clickable toasts that pan the camera to the fire (M19 polish), fire from power plant
  breakdowns, traffic accidents.
