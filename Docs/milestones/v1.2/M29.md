# M29 — Age pacing (plan, 2026-10-16)

*Part of the 1.2 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md). Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13
(Ages & research, "Research costs (M21a/b/e)"); procedure: `balance-tuning` skill.*

## Goal
Advancing to the next age costs noticeably more research, so each age is lived in longer and the advance is a goal,
not an afterthought.

## Current numbers (as read 2026-10-16)
Advance project costs (`Tools/gen_age_content.py` `AGES` × `ADVANCE_COST_SCALE` 1 / 1 / 2 / 2.5): **Renaissance 250,
Industrial 2,400, Modern 7,500 RP.** Single techs cost far more than the advance into the Renaissance (×12 since M21e:
Medieval 360–1,080, Renaissance 1,920–3,120). Each age also needs `TechsToAdvance` techs (5 / 4 / 5) and a population
(120 / 350 / 650). Engaged player (budget on): Medieval 199 days, Renaissance 168, Industrial 192, Modern on day 559.

## Design decisions (answered 2026-10-16: the proposed costs accepted)
| Topic | Default | Why / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| What changes | Only the advance project's RP cost | Alternatives: more `TechsToAdvance`, a higher population gate, or a lower RP income. The cost is content-only (no code, no save change) and is what the feedback names. |
| How much | **Accepted:** advance costs **Renaissance 2,000, Industrial 6,000, Modern 15,000 RP** (≈ ×8 / ×2.5 / ×2): each advance ≈ two to three of that age's techs | Target: about **+25–35% days per age** (Medieval ~250, Renaissance ~210, Industrial ~240, Modern near day 700). Bigger or smaller is one table edit; the probe gives the days before the numbers are fixed. |
| Tutorial | Unchanged in feel: the tutorial city already researches ×12 (`TutorialResearchBoost`); check its 120-day bound still holds, raise the boost only if needed | The tutorial ends at the Renaissance, so the Renaissance advance is the one it feels. |
| Test bounds | Re-record `AgeBalanceTests` windows from the probe (Medieval and later-age ranges, "Modern by day 1000" → 1,200 if needed); happiness / money floors unchanged | This milestone deliberately changes the timeline; the age-less baseline and `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` are unchanged (a city that starts in Industrial never pays an earlier advance). |

## Steps
- **29a. Probe.** `ResearchProbe` with candidate scales (a temporary table in the probe, not the assets): days per age,
  RP/day at each advance, lowest happiness. Confirm the accepted costs land near the +25–35% target; report the days, and only revisit the numbers if they are far off.
- **29b. Content + tests.** Edit `AGES` / `ADVANCE_COST_SCALE`, re-run the generator, commit the rewritten `Ages/*.asset`;
  re-record `AgeBalanceTests` ranges (all variants: budget, density Gated / Mixed, disasters), `TutorialPlaythroughTests`,
  `ContentTests` green.
- **29c. UI check + docs.** The research panel's advance row shows the new cost and a sensible days-left estimate
  (Play-mode screenshot); GamePlan §13 Research costs line, §12 status, `Docs/Changelog.md` entry drafted for M31.

## Risks / open questions
- A longer age means the city outgrows its services for longer while the next techs come; M21 saw happiness lows of
  0.41–0.44. If the lows fall below the 0.40 floor, tune the services the engaged player builds, not the floor.
- Players with saves mid-advance keep their progress (research progress is saved RP; a higher cost just means more to go).
