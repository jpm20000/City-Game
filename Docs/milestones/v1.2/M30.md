# M30 — Low-density look (plan, 2026-10-16) — done (2026-10-16)

*Part of the 1.2 roadmap: [`Roadmap.md`](Roadmap.md). Where it fits: `../../GamePlan.md` §12; systems as built: §13
(Art & atmosphere, Density); procedures: `scene-prefab-editing` (generated content), `perf-benchmark`.*

## Goal
Low-density blocks read as their own kind of place in every age:
- **Residential:** small single homes that get nicer by level (L1 cottage → L2 house with garden → L3 larger house
  with porch / extension).
- **Commercial:** open **markets** at L1 / L2 (stalls, then a covered market), a **single-storey shop** at L3.
- **Industrial:** **farmland** at every level (fields, then barn, then barn + silo / farmhouse).

## Current state (as read 2026-10-16)
`AgeVisualSet` has Low tables for R / C / I (`m_ResidentialLow`, `m_CommercialLow`, `m_IndustrialLow`); a slot with no
prefab draws the Medium style at 0.6 height (`GrowthVisuals.k_LowHeight`). `KitDesigns.ForDensity` returns art only for
the **Modern** age and only for R and C (6 specs: garden flats, low shops); every other age, and Low industry
everywhere, is the squashed Medium look. Low is the only density in the Medieval age (`MediumDensityMinAge` 1), so the
Medieval town is entirely Low — this milestone changes how the whole early game looks. Medieval max level is 2.

## Design decisions (answered 2026-10-16: farms pollute less)
| Topic | Default | Why / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Ages covered | All four, same brief, the age's materials (Medieval timber / daub / thatch, Renaissance stone / terracotta, Industrial brick / slate, Modern render / tile / glass) | Alternative: one age-neutral set. Per-age keeps M11's "buildings show their age". |
| Modern Low (M23 garden flats / low shops) | Replaced by the new brief (bungalows, a market hall / kiosks, a one-storey shop) | Keeping them would contradict "markets at L1/L2". |
| Farmland in the sim | **Low industry pollutes less** (asked): a `BalanceConfig.LowDensityPollution` multiplier (default 0.3, "(tuned, M30)") in `PollutionSystem`, beside the existing `HighDensityPollution` (1.25); jobs and goods unchanged; selection panel / hover name it "Farm" | Art only — not taken. 0.3 is a starting value for the 30a probe. |
| Farm crops | Crop rows in 3–4 colours (wheat, green crop, ploughed soil, pasture) varying by variant; fields fill most of the cell | — |
| Art contract heights | Low tables get their own minimum heights (L1 0.03, L2 0.15, L3 0.25 cells) so flat fields and one-storey shops pass `ArtContract` | The current minimums (0.15 / 0.40 / 0.60) were set for Medium; a field cannot be 0.6 tall. |

## Content table (per age, three variants each)
| Slot | L1 | L2 | L3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| R Low | one-room cottage, tiny plot | house with garden fence / hedge | larger house: two-storey or wing, porch, chimney(s) |
| C Low | 1–2 open stalls (posts + awning), crates | covered market (open-sided roof on posts, several stalls) | single-storey shop, shopfront, awning, sign |
| I Low | field with crop rows + small shed | fields + barn | fields + barn + silo (Industrial: windpump; Modern: greenhouse / grain bins) |

## Architecture
- **Sim (pure, Simulation asmdef):** `PollutionSystem` line `density = High ? HighDensityPollution : 1` becomes a
  three-way pick with `LowDensityPollution`; `BalanceConfig` field + asset value (`BalanceTests` code default = asset).
  The age-less sim and the seeded-city tests zone Medium everywhere, so their numbers are unchanged; the M23 density
  runs of `AgeBalanceTests` (`EngagedCity.DensityUse` Gated / Mixed) do change — Medieval industry is all Low there —
  and are re-recorded. New tests: a Low factory emits `LowDensityPollution` × a Medium one; land value and happiness
  near a farm are better than near a Medium factory.
- **Palette** (`KitPalette.Entries`): append crop / soil / hedge / fence colours at the end (earlier indices never move,
  as with the M18f vehicle lights).
- **Kit features** (`KitSpec` + `BuildingKitGenerator.Compose` / `KitMeshBuilder`): `Field` (thin slab with N crop rows,
  colours per variant, `FieldCover` fraction), `Fence` / `Hedge` (low border), `Stalls` (count; post + canopy boxes,
  open sides, reuse the awning stripes), `OpenRoof` (roof on posts, no walls), `Silo` (cylinder + cap, reuse the stack /
  tank builder), `Porch`, `Garden` (a green slab behind the house). The house body sits on part of the cell when
  `Field` / `Garden` is used (`BodyOffsetZ`).
- **Designs**: `KitDesigns.ForDensity(age, Density.Low)` returns 9 specs (slot = zone × 3 + level − 1, R / C / I) for
  every age; the generator loop already supports `zone = slot / 3` up to 3 zones, and writes `m_IndustrialLow` because
  the field name is built from `s_ZoneFields[zone] + density`.
- **Contract**: `ArtContract` takes per-table minimum heights (Low uses the lower row); `ContentTests` keeps validating
  every prefab.
- Generated output: `Generate Building Kit` menu → meshes / prefabs under the kit folders; commit the `.meta`s Unity
  writes. 4 ages × 9 slots × 3 variants = 108 Low prefabs (Modern's 18 existing Low prefabs replaced).

## Risks / open questions
- Triangle count: fields with many rows and stalls with posts add geometry on the most common Medieval block; budget
  ~2× today's Medieval Low triangles and keep rows as a few long boxes.
- Flat fields hide behind tall neighbours at the 30° camera; the variety has to come from colour more than height.
- The title-screen showcase city (`StreamingAssets/showcase.json`) will look different; check it still reads well.
