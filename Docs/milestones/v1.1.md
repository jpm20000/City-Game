# Chronopolis 1.1 — roadmap (M20–M26) (plan, 2026-10-05)

*Roadmap for the first update after 1.0.0. Each milestone gets its own full plan in `Docs/milestones/Mxx.md`
when it starts (`milestone-workflow`); this file holds the outlines, the order and the decisions that shape
more than one milestone. Where it fits: `../GamePlan.md` §12 (stub), systems as built: §13.*

## Order and why

Feedback items marked **IMPORTANT** come first and all land in **M20**. They are all interface work (camera,
toolbar, New City dialog) and touch no sim numbers and no save format. They ship with the rest of 1.1 (no early
1.0.1, asked 2026-10-05).

The rest is ordered by size and dependency: small balance and feedback fixes first, then roads (save format),
then zoning density and the Modern skyline (save format, art), then Goods (a new sim system that changes the
baseline), and last custom assets, which builds on the M20 toolbar groups and the M23 art.

## M20 — Camera & toolbar (IMPORTANT) — full plan: [`M20.md`](M20.md)

Rotatable camera (90° snaps, Q / E), the Buildings section of the toolbar folds into **group buttons with
flyouts** (Services › police, fire, health, schools; Utilities › power, water; Parks; Research; …), the New City
dialog's Disasters & events and Guided tutorial switches get a visible on/off box (they currently show no state),
and the View section folds the same way (one Views flyout). No sim, save or balance change.

## M21 — Progression & feedback

- **Research costs.** Measure first: an `[Explicit]` probe printing, per age, days to research each tech and to
  advance with `EngagedCity` (and the RP sources at that point). Likely levers in `BalanceConfig` / content: a
  per-age tech cost multiplier, a softer RP per commercial worker as population grows (diminishing returns), or
  research buildings' RP capped by population. Target: each age takes noticeably longer than today (numbers set in
  the M21 plan from the probe). Re-record `AgeBalanceTests` timings and the tutorial's ≤ 120-day harness bound
  (Medieval research must not slow the tutorial past it).
- **Paved before avenues.** Check whether Electric Trams (avenue) can be researched before Macadam (paved) on an
  advance from the Renaissance (Macadam is an Industrial *starting* tech, so a city that advances into the
  Industrial age may not get it). Fix in `Tools/gen_age_content.py`: Macadam a prerequisite of Electric Trams (and
  granted on advance if starting techs are not). `ContentTests` stay green.
- **Repair icon.** A pooled world-space icon (billboard sprite on one shared material, on the `Buildings` layer
  height) over every broken-down source (`DisasterSystem.Broken`), drawn by a new `StatusIcons` presenter that reads
  the sim. Built so more statuses can be added later (on fire, no power, no water, no road) without new systems.
  Clicking the building still opens the Repair button in `SelectionPanel`.

## M22 — Road layout (save v8)

- **2-wide avenues.** The Avenue tool previews and lays a 2-cell strip (the second lane on the drag's right-hand
  side, flipped with R), refuses where the pair doesn't fit, upgrades a street to an avenue only when the
  neighbouring lane can be added. Art: `RoadTilemapView` draws the median between paired avenue cells. Capacity per
  cell re-tuned so a 2-wide avenue carries about what a 1-wide one did plus the width bonus. Demolishing one lane
  demolishes the pair.
- **One-way highways.** `GridData` gains a per-road direction byte (0 = two-way; N / E / S / W), saved in v8
  (migration: existing highways become two-way so old cities keep working, with a toast offering nothing).
  `TrafficSystem` / `RoadNetwork` build a directed graph for highway cells; placement sets direction from the drag;
  arrows on highway tiles; vehicles follow direction; a "Reverse direction" action in `SelectionPanel`. Ramps =
  any highway cell meeting a street (no interchanges in 1.1).
- Watch `PerfBenchmark` (directed Dial's algorithm on 96²).

## M23 — Density & Modern skyline (save v9)

- **Density** per zoned cell (`byte`, survives `GridData.Resize`, cleared with the zone): Low / Medium / High,
  **chosen by the player when zoning and never changed by the sim** — growth only moves a building through levels
  1–3 *inside* its density. Capacity = `CapacityModel` (zone, level, density). Medium = today's capacities
  (identity, so the seeded city and `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` keep their numbers); Low and High are
  their own bands (Low: +land value, −traffic; High: needs water + power, more traffic and pollution). High is
  unlocked per age (Renaissance for C, Industrial for R / I, Modern for skyscrapers and office blocks); until then
  the High button is hidden. Repainting a built cell to a lower density redevelops it into that band (with a
  confirmation when it would evict people / jobs). Growth explanations via `GrowthSystem` + `GrowthBlocker` as
  today; no new density gate is needed because density never grows.
- **UI:** a Low / Medium / High selector next to the zone buttons (remembered per session, default Medium), the
  density in `SelectionPanel`, and a Density info view (or a mode of the Zones view).
- **Art:** the building kit gains density variants. Modern Medium L3 residential (apartment block) and commercial
  (shops with offices above) are redesigned to read differently in shape and colour and not as towers; Modern High
  residential = **Skyscrapers**, Modern High commercial = **Office blocks** (glass, flat tops), multi-cell footprints
  optional (decision in the M23 plan). Re-run `PerfBenchmark` (taller meshes, more variants).
- `EngagedCity` paints density; `AgeBalanceTests` both ways.

## M24 — Goods (save v10)

- **Sim.** Industry produces goods per worker (per age, Industrial techs raise it); commercial sells goods (its
  jobs and income need supply); homes need a commercial cell within reach that has stock, or lose happiness and
  can't reach the top level. City-wide pool first (simplest, testable), with a reach / traffic term only if the
  pool alone feels flat. A deficit imports goods at a price (Budget ledger line); a surplus exports for income.
- **Identity:** with goods content absent (the age-less sim), supply = demand, so the legacy tests keep their
  numbers. With the shipped content the Industrial baseline changes; the M24 plan states the new numbers and why
  (M12 precedent).
- **UI:** goods meter in the HUD (under demand), Goods view (produce / consume per cell), ledger lines,
  `GrowthBlocker.NoGoods`, happiness tooltip line. Tutorial text if Medieval is affected (it should not be: goods
  could start in the Renaissance, decision in the plan).
- `EngagedCity` balances industry vs commercial; balance tests with goods on, three seeds.

## M25 — Custom assets

- **Guidance:** `Docs/CustomAssets.md`: the art contract (`ArtContract.cs`: 1×1 pivot, facing +Z to the street,
  height, `Kit.mat` or own URP Simple Lit material), how to add a growable variant (an `AgeVisualSet` slot) vs a
  placeable building (`BuildingDefinition` + prefab + `RequiredTech`), import settings, testing in Play mode.
- **Editor wizard:** *CityBuilder › Add Custom Building…*: pick a model / prefab, choose growable (age × zone ×
  level × density) or placeable (cost, upkeep, effects), it writes the assets, validates against the art contract
  and adds it to the database.
- **Searchable placement menu:** a panel (hotkey + toolbar button) listing every unlocked placeable and decoration
  with a search box, filters by group / age, and custom items tagged "Custom". Custom growables can optionally be
  *placed* as a ploppable at a chosen level (a "landmark" that counts as that zone's capacity). Builds on M20's
  groups.

## M26 — Release 1.1

Version 1.1.0, Windows release (`Build Windows Release`, `-smokeTest`), WebGL build (check camera rotation, flyouts
and the search box under `#if UNITY_WEBGL`), changelog, perf benchmark against the M18 / M19 budget, a full
virtual-input play-through from the main menu touching each 1.1 feature, and the docs status lines.

## Risks across 1.1

- **Camera rotation** touches everything that assumes one view: picking, the pan axes, `ApplyBounds`, light tones,
  sorting, the showcase drift, building-face art (back faces were never seen). Isolated in M20 so later milestones
  build on it.
- **Save versions 8–10** in three milestones: each adds one migration step with a test; the v1 fixture must keep
  migrating.
- **Goods** is the only milestone that changes the real-content baseline; it goes after the cheaper milestones so
  a slip doesn't hold the rest of 1.1.
