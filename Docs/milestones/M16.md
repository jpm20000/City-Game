# M16 — Traffic (plan, 2026-10-04)

*Live plan — update step notes here as steps are done. Where it fits: `../GamePlan.md` §12 (stub + status), systems as built: §13.*


**Goal:** roads stop being free pipes. Every day the city's workers commute from their homes to the
jobs. This is a **statistical flow**: no agents, one pass over the road graph. It loads each road cell
with trips. A road carrying more than its tier's capacity is **congested**. Homes whose commute runs
through a jam lose happiness, and cells beside a jammed road lose land value (which can hold back
level-3 upgrades through the M12 gate). Roads come in **five tiers** (dirt track → cobbled street →
paved road → avenue → highway). Each tier is unlocked by a tech and has its own cost, upkeep, capacity
and travel speed. Highways carry the most but give **no frontage**. The player upgrades busy streets by
dragging a better tier over them, and a **Traffic view** shows where the city jams.

**Done when:**
- `GridData` stores a road **tier** per cell (a byte, 0 = no road). The road tool lays the best
  unlocked street tier, and the Avenue / Highway tools appear once Electric Trams / Automobiles are
  researched. Dragging a better tier over a road upgrades it for the price difference. Each tier is
  drawn differently by `RoadTilemapView`.
- Each tick computes the trips on every road cell from the homes → jobs flow. On a 96² city this takes
  ≤ 0.3 ms in EditMode, and the `PerfBenchmark` sim tick stays within 0.3 ms of the M15 numbers.
- Congestion shows up where the player looks: a **Traffic** happiness term (tooltip), a **Traffic**
  line in the land-value breakdown (and so the level-3 gate), the Traffic view (roads green → red by
  load / capacity, homes by commute congestion), `SelectionPanel` (road: tier, trips / capacity;
  home: commute congestion and its cost; zoned land beside only a highway: "highways give no access")
  and a one-time "roads are jamming" toast.
- Highways connect road networks and the map edge, but cells beside them get no road access.
- The ledger's Roads line is the sum of each tier's upkeep. In the age-less sim every road is Paved
  and costs exactly what it does today (`RoadCost` $50, `RoadUpkeepPerDay` $1).
- Save v5 stores road tiers in the existing `Roads` bytes. v4 (and the v1 fixture) migrate (old roads
  become the best street tier the save has researched; Paved without ages). Save → load → continue
  equals an uninterrupted run.
- Traffic runs in every sim, including the age-less one. The seeded 24² city at day 60 and
  `IndustrialStart_RealContent_Baseline` (220 / 0.631) stay unchanged. If a later seeded-city day
  jams, its numbers are re-recorded with a note (the decision below). `AgeBalanceTests` hold
  (each age in 45–90 days from Medieval, never in debt, happiness ≥ 0.55) with the engaged player
  upgrading jammed roads, with and without `UseBudget`. All EditMode tests are green, and a UI-only
  virtual-input play-through passes.

## Design decisions (defaults — change any before the step that uses them)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Baseline | **Asked 2026-10-04: traffic on everywhere.** The age-less sim gets traffic too (it is the Industrial game). All its roads are Paved, and Paved's capacity is tuned so the seeded 24² city at day 60 stays below `CongestionFree` (no number moves). Any seeded-city day that does jam (the day-90 / day-120 runs at 330+ pop are the candidates) is re-recorded in its step note, with old and new numbers. |
| Highways | **Asked 2026-10-04: no frontage.** `RoadNetwork.HasRoadAccess` ignores neighbouring tiers without `Frontage`, so a zoned cell beside only a highway never grows (`GrowthBlocker.NoRoadAccess`, with the panel text "highways give no access"). Highways still count for connectivity (the map-edge entry BFS), and they carry power and water like any road. |
| Congestion effects | **Asked 2026-10-04: happiness + land value, no new growth gate.** A **Traffic** `HappinessBreakdown` term from each home's commute congestion, and a **Traffic** `LandValueBreakdown` line from the jam on the roads beside a cell. A jam holds level-3 homes and shops through the existing `LowLandValue` gate; `SelectionPanel.LandValueFixes` names "upgrade the jammed road". |
| Tier storage | `GridData`'s `bool[] m_Roads` becomes `byte[] m_RoadTiers` (0 = none, 1..5 = tier). `IsRoad` = tier > 0. New: `GetRoadTier` / `SetRoadTier` (0 removes, > 0 clears a pipe, raises `OnCellChanged` on any change), `CountRoadsByTier(int[])`. `SetRoad(cell, true)` lays `GridData.DefaultRoadTier` (default 3 = Paved, so every existing test and legacy call site lays today's road), and `SetRoad(cell, false)` removes it. `ExportRoads` / `Import` carry tiers. A tier change on a road changes no carrying flag, so the `UtilityNetwork` topology is not rebuilt (`RoadNetwork` only cares when frontage changes). |
| Tier data | `RoadTierDefinition` (Simulation asmdef ScriptableObject: `Tier` byte, Id, DisplayName, `RequiredTech`, `Cost`, `UpkeepPerDay`, `Capacity`, `TravelCost`, `Frontage`, `ObsoleteBy` = the tier that replaces it in the toolbar). It is listed in **`TechDatabase.RoadTiers`** (both databases or neither, like ordinances); `Validate` checks the tiers are 1..5, unique, with a tech that exists. `RoadTiers` (pure, `SimulationSystem.RoadTiers`) is a 6-entry lookup table built from the database, or **legacy** without one: every tier = Paved with the `BalanceConfig` road values. `IsUnlocked(tier)` (tech researched; dirt has none), `BestStreetTier` (best unlocked tier with frontage that is not obsolete), `UpgradeCost(from, to) = max(0, cost(to) − cost(from))`. |
| Commuting model | Trips per grown home cell = residents in it (capacity × the city's occupancy, `Population / Housing`) × the employment rate (`Employed / Workers`) × `TripsPerWorker` (1.0) × `TechModifiers.TrafficMultiplier`. **Sinks** = road cells with frontage beside a grown commercial or industrial cell (distance 0), plus the map-edge entry roads at `OutsideTripCost` (40). The edge is a fallback, so a network without jobs still commutes "out of town". A multi-source **Dial's algorithm** (bucket queue, integer `TravelCost` per cell entered) gives every road cell its distance to the nearest sink and a parent step towards it. Seeds are row-major and neighbours go in `CellUtils.Neighbors4` order, so ties are deterministic. Each home puts its trips on its best frontage road (lowest distance, row-major tie-break). Loads accumulate down the tree in decreasing-distance order (each cell passes its load to its parent), so `Load(road)` = trips through that cell. A second pass in increasing order gives each road its **commute congestion** = the worst `load / capacity` from it to its sink, and a home's commute congestion = that value at its road. O(roads + homes) per tick, with no per-home path walks. |
| Why nearest-sink | It is cheap and deterministic, and it puts load where a player would expect it: on the streets between homes and the nearest shops and works. Jobs' capacity is ignored, so a big residential district beside one small shop sends all its trips there. The 16b probe checks how this reads in the engaged city. The fallback, if it looks wrong, is to seed each sink at `JobSinkCost / jobs` so bigger job clusters pull from further away. That is still one pass. |
| When it runs | In `Tick` after Growth and the second `RecountCapacity` (so the employment rate is today's), before `MeasureServices` (which reads the commute congestion). `TrafficSystem` keeps its arrays across ticks. A cached **topology** (each home's frontage roads, each road's tier capacity / cost) is rebuilt only when roads or tiers change, the way `UtilityNetwork` does it. The flow itself runs every tick (levels change daily). Derived, never saved: after a load, `SimulationSystem.Restore` recomputes it, so the UI and save → continue match. |
| Happiness | `ServiceStats.TrafficPenalty` = housing-weighted average over grown homes of `TrafficPenaltyAt(config, congestion)` = `min(max(0, congestion − CongestionFree) × TrafficPenalty, TrafficPenaltyCap)`, with `CongestionFree` 0.8, `TrafficPenalty` 0.10 and cap 0.08 (a commute at 160% costs 8%). → `HappinessBreakdown.Traffic` (optional constructor arg, in `Total`). Not ramped (a small town doesn't jam). |
| Land value | `− min(max(0, local − CongestionFree) × LandValuePerCongestion 0.10, LandValueCongestionCap 0.10)`, where `local` = the highest `load / capacity` among the cell's 4-neighbour roads (noise and fumes from the street outside, not the commute). → `LandValueBreakdown.Traffic`. `LandValueSystem` gets an optional `traffic` argument (null = no line). |
| Tier content | Table below. Paved = the legacy road (cost, upkeep and capacity from `BalanceConfig`, mirrored in its asset, so the age-less sim and an Industrial start lay the same road). Dirt needs no tech, so a Medieval start can build. Cobble comes from Architecture, Paved from a **new tech Macadam** (Industrial, 150 RP, an Industrial **starting** tech like Electricity / Waterworks, so an Industrial start keeps today's road), Avenue from Electric Trams, Highway from Automobiles. Street tiers obsolete each other in the toolbar (dirt → cobble → paved); Avenue and Highway stay. Placed roads never change by themselves. |
| New effect type | `TechEffectType.TrafficMultiplier` (append only): trips × Value. Railways ×0.85 ("goods and commuters by rail"; its first content), Electric Trams ×0.9 (+ Avenue), Automobiles ×1.2 (+ Highway; also commercial demand ×1.1 so the tech is not only a cost), Car-free Sundays gains ×0.9. Smart Grid stays effect-less (a candidate for M17's plant breakdowns). |
| Road tool / upgrade | `PlacementController.SelectRoad(tier)` (`B` = the best street tier). Laying a road on empty land pays `Cost(tier)`. Dragging over a road of a lower tier upgrades it for `UpgradeCost`, with one insufficient-funds toast per drag, like pipes. The same or a higher tier is skipped (hint "Already Avenue"). Downgrading only happens through demolish + relay; demolishing a road refunds nothing (as today). Hints: "Paved road $50", "Upgrade to Avenue $100", "Highway — no access for blocks beside it". |
| Saves | **v5**: the `Roads` bytes hold tiers (no new field). `SaveMigrations.V4ToV5` maps every road byte 1 to the best street tier unlocked by the save's `Researched` list (Paved for `NoAge`, after the v1→v2 adoption the city is Industrial, so the v1 fixture gets Paved). `SaveSystem` validation rejects road bytes > 5. Version bump because the bytes' meaning changes. |
| Toasts | Once per city the first time the Traffic term passes −1%: "Roads are jamming — upgrade the busiest streets (Traffic view)". Research complete names the road tier unlocked ("Avenues unlocked"). The Industrial welcome toast is unchanged (an Industrial start already has Paved; a Medieval city reaching Industrial researches Macadam like Electricity). |
| UI | Toolbar Transport: the existing Road button shows the **best street tier** (label, cost and tooltip follow it; refreshed on `TechCompleted` / `CityLoaded`). **Avenue** and **Highway** buttons are created at runtime (copies of the Road button, `Road_avenue` / `Road_highway`, hidden while locked; the toolbar already scales itself down when it overflows). `InfoOverlay.View.Traffic` ("Traffic" VIEW button, always available, after Value in the `V` cycle; the Avenue / Highway tools switch to it, the street tool doesn't). `SelectionPanel`, `HappinessTooltip` "Traffic", land-value line and fixes. `TechPanel` unlock lists get road tiers from the descriptions ("Unlocks the Avenue."). |

## Architecture

**Pure (Grid and Simulation asmdefs):**

| New / changed | Notes |
|---|---|
| `GridData` (Grid asmdef) | Byte tiers, `GetRoadTier` / `SetRoadTier`, `DefaultRoadTier` (3), `CountRoadsByTier`, export / import of tiers. `IsRoad` / `SetRoad` / `CountRoads` keep their meaning. |
| `RoadTierDefinition` (new), `TechDatabase.RoadTiers` | Data + validation (tiers 1..5 unique, tech exists, costs ≥ 0, capacity > 0, travel cost ≥ 1). |

| `RoadNetwork` | Optional frontage lookup (`Func<byte, bool>`, null = every road gives access). `HasRoadAccess` skips neighbours without frontage, `IsConnectedToEntry` unchanged; the cache goes dirty on any road change (already does). |
| `TrafficSystem` (new, `SimulationSystem.Traffic`) | Topology cache (frontage roads per home, capacity / cost per road; rebuilt on road / tier / zone changes) + the daily flow (`Update(tripsPerResident)`). `Load(cell)`, `Capacity(cell)`, `Congestion(cell)` = load / capacity, `CommuteCongestion(home)`, `LocalCongestion(cell)`, `Trips` (total), `JammedRoads` (count > 1.0). Flat int arrays, a bucket queue, reallocates on `OnResized`. Derived, never saved. |
| `TechEffectType` / `TechModifiers` | `+ TrafficMultiplier`. |
| `ServiceStats` / `HappinessBreakdown` / `PopulationSystem` | `TrafficPenalty` (measured with the rest; optional `traffic`, null = 0) → `+ Traffic` term. |
| `LandValueSystem` / `LandValueBreakdown` | Optional `traffic`; `+ Traffic` line. |
| `SimulationSystem` | Owns `RoadTiers` + `Traffic`; `Tick` runs the flow after growth; `Ledger()` Roads = `RoadTiers.UpkeepPerDay(grid)` (× the tech upkeep multiplier as today); sets `GridData.DefaultRoadTier` to the best street tier (Paved without ages); `Restore` recomputes traffic. |
| `BalanceConfig` | `RoadCapacity` 160, `RoadTravelCost` 4, `TripsPerWorker` 1.0, `OutsideTripCost` 40, `CongestionFree` 0.8, `TrafficPenalty` 0.10, `TrafficPenaltyCap` 0.08, `LandValuePerCongestion` 0.10, `LandValueCongestionCap` 0.10, tagged "(M16)" and mirrored in the asset. |
| `SaveData` / `SaveMigrations` / `SaveSystem` | `CurrentVersion` 5, `V4ToV5` (road bytes → tiers), validation of tier bytes. |

## Risks / open questions

- **Nearest-sink flow concentrates load.** Every trip goes to the nearest job road by travel cost, so
  one shop beside a big housing estate looks like a traffic magnet. Probe in 16b, and use the
  `JobSinkCost / jobs` seeding fallback (still one pass) if it reads wrong in the Traffic view.
- **A 1-cell road with a per-cell capacity** means junction cells near the job district always carry
  the most. That's intended (the place to put an avenue). Watch that it doesn't make the junction beside
  the map edge (the outside fallback) the city's worst cell in every save: `OutsideTripCost` 40 keeps
  the edge a last resort.
- **The baseline may move after day 60.** The seeded city at 330+ pop (day 90 / 120) might jam on its
  single cross. The decision accepts re-recording those, but if day 60 jams, raise `RoadCapacity`
  instead of re-recording.
- **Cheaper Medieval roads speed up the Medieval age** (84 of 90 days today, so faster is safe). The
  upkeep difference is small; the probe records it.
- **Toolbar space at 1920:** two more Transport buttons and one more VIEW button. The toolbar already
  scales itself down. If the labels become unreadable, fold Avenue / Highway into a small tier strip
  that opens from the Road button (decide in 16c from a screenshot).
- **Highways without frontage confuse** the player the first time a zone beside one stays empty. The
  hint on the tool, the zone overlay's "no access" stripes and the blocker text all say why.
- **Tier upgrades and the utility topology:** a tier change must not rebuild power / water topology
  every drag step (it changes no carrying flag). 16a has a test for it, and 16c checks a 96² drag.
- **Engaged player never builds highways,** so `AgeBalanceTests` don't exercise them. They're covered
  by unit tests and the play-through. Revisit if Modern balance needs them.
- **Not in M16 (later candidates):** traffic pollution along busy roads, shopping / freight trips,
  public transit buildings, visual cars (M18's optional extra reads `TrafficSystem.Load`).
